Razorpay · COD Remittance Recovery

Couriers remit. Razorpay checks. You claim.

For D2C brands on Magic Checkout that ship cash-on-delivery through Shiprocket. Shiprocket collects the cash at the door and pays it back days later in one bank transfer covering hundreds of orders, so a missing or short-paid order slips through. After every payout, the app checks each order against the brand's own Razorpay order record. If money is missing, it hands the founder a ready claim. COD goes from Shiprocket to the brand's bank, and Razorpay never holds it.
